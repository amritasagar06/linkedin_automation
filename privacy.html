Privacy Policy — Interview Content Desk

This personal app helps its owner draft technical articles and LinkedIn posts. It stores interview questions, draft text, writing-style examples, and encrypted LinkedIn OAuth tokens in a private Supabase project. It uses an AI text-generation API to create drafts and a Telegram bot to notify the owner when a draft is ready.

The app uses LinkedIn permissions only to identify the connected member and publish a post after the owner approves it. It does not sell or share account credentials. Drafts and tokens are accessible only to the app owner and the app's server-side services.

The owner can delete saved drafts, style examples, or disconnect LinkedIn by deleting the corresponding records or revoking the app in LinkedIn settings. For questions about this personal app, contact the app owner through the contact information on the app's LinkedIn profile.

Use of LinkedIn and other connected services is subject to their own privacy policies and terms.
